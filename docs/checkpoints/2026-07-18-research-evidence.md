# 2026-07-18 research evidence checkpoint

The current redesign is based on two direct browser studies plus institutional guidance.

- The Art of Cinema: desktop top, full-page, and fixed-width mobile behavior were captured.
- ZUTOMAYO: desktop, mobile, and a before/after drag state were captured.
- The reference screenshots are evidence only. No third-party image, font, logo, copy, frame asset,
  or source code is part of the runtime.
- Institutional sources reviewed: RCA, UAL, Yale School of Art, Vitsœ/Dieter Rams, Bauhaus-Archiv,
  Cooper Hewitt, Letterform Archive, MoMA, W3C WCAG 2.2, and W3C JLREQ.
- The new expression retains three interface colors and uses CIBA-owned project media only.

Visual records are stored in `docs/design-references/research-2026-07-18/`.
